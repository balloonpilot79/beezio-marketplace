01. Start in your Business Center

Choose your business role.

1. Use Seller, Affiliate, or Ambassador to find the tools for your goal.
2. Choose Customize My Store to build your storefront.
3. My Referral Links & Invites opens your ambassador sharing tools.

Open this step on Beezio →

02. Make your store yours

Add your name, description, and branding.

1. Set your store name, URL slug, and a short description.
2. Choose a look and check the live preview. Add a logo or banner in Images and color palette below.
Save changes, then Preview live store before sharing your store URL.

Open this step on Beezio →

03. List a product you sell

Business Center > Add product

1. Enter a clear product title.
2. Describe the item accurately, including condition and what is included.
3. Select the category. Complete the condition and merchandise fields below when relevant.

Open this step on Beezio →

04. Add quantity and real photos

Help customers see what they are buying.

1. Set the quantity you can supply. Use condition notes for wear, missing pieces, or repairs.
2. Upload clear product images. Show the actual item or accurately labeled product mockups.
For sizes and colors, use the variants section further down the form.

Open this step on Beezio →

05. Set payouts and shipping

Complete the listing before saving.

1. Enter your seller ask and the affiliate payout you choose. Review the calculated customer price.
2. Enter shipping expense, production time if needed, and estimated delivery time.
3. Write shipping and return terms. Review the form and choose Save Product. You fulfill your own product orders.

Open this step on Beezio →

06. Find products to promote

Affiliate path: Marketplace > Business tools

1. Switch the marketplace to Business tools.
2. Open promotion kit to prepare tracked product sharing.
3. Browse categories and eligible products. Use Add to My Store where available to build your affiliate store.
The seller fulfills their products; your affiliate earnings depend on qualifying referred sales.

Open this step on Beezio →

07. Share a product with pictures

Promotion kit > choose a product

Select a product, then choose a square post, pin, story, or flyer. Download the picture and use your own Copy link or Copy caption controls.

Images alone are not clickable. Include the tracked link in your post, profile, or other supported link field. Always use the link generated inside your own account.

Open this step on Beezio →

08. Invite businesses as an ambassador

Business Center > My Referral Links & Invites

1. Copy your own personal invitation link. Use Text invite or Messenger when useful.
2. Download your QR for flyers or copy the ready-to-share invitation.
People register through your link so their referral can attach to you. Earnings come from qualifying sales, not signup alone.

Open this step on Beezio →

09. Save, preview, and take one step

Your first launch checklist

Save your store changes and preview the live store. Publish one product, add one eligible affiliate product, or send one helpful ambassador invitation.

Use Orders to manage sales and fulfillment. Use Earnings / Financials to review balances and payout requirements. Check account messages and order updates regularly.
