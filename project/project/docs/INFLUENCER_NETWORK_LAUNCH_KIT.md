# Beezio Influencer Network — Launch Kit (Draft)

## Positioning
**Affiliates sell products. Influencers bring sellers and affiliates who sell products.**

Headline: **You bring the people. They make the sales. You earn.**

Approved-for-review claim: "Invite sellers and affiliates to Beezio. Earn from their qualifying sales while the accounts, referrals, and program remain eligible." Do not imply guaranteed lifetime income or guaranteed sales. No public payout amounts.

## Proposed landing page /influencers
- Hero: "Build connections. Earn from sales."
- Subhead: "Refer sellers and affiliates to Beezio. When they make qualifying sales, you may earn too."
- CTAs: "Join Beezio free" -> existing business registration; "Already a member? Get your referral link" -> existing influencer tools.
- Three steps: Join free; share tracked referral link; earn from qualifying sales.
- Explain influencer vs affiliate in one sentence.
- FAQ: No inventory required to recruit; no payment for signups alone; no guaranteed income; referrals and earnings subject to Influencer Terms; disclose financial relationships in posts.
- Link to /legal/influencer-terms (verify exact live route before implementation).
- Preserve existing referral attribution, two-slot logic, fees, payouts, and checkout untouched.

## Recruiting post — side hustle communities
**Looking for a free way to build an online income opportunity?**

Beezio lets sellers open free online stores and affiliates promote products. We're also inviting influencers who can introduce sellers and affiliates to the platform.

As a Beezio influencer, you get a tracked referral link. When eligible people you refer generate qualifying sales, you may earn a referral bonus. There's no inventory purchase or membership fee required for influencer participation.

Earnings aren't guaranteed and depend on actual qualifying sales. I may earn compensation from eligible referrals.

Learn more: https://beezio.co

Only post where group rules permit promotions. Use the actual personal referral link when sharing.

## First 72 hours
0h: Welcome + tracked link + disclosure reminder + copy/share assets.
24h: Suggest first post and 3 relevant personal invitations (no spam).
48h: Show referral activity if supported; suggest second creative.
72h: Encourage follow-up, not repeated mass posting.
Day 7: Show signups, qualifying sales and earnings where data exists.

## Recognition proposal (no commission changes)
- Founding Influencer: early participation badge
- Beezio Builder: 10 verified eligible referred accounts
- Beezio Champion: 50 verified eligible referred accounts
All creators qualify on identical terms. Recognition is nonfinancial; avoid promising preferential compensation.

## Measurement
Record landing-page visits, registration starts/completions, referral-link shares, verified referred accounts, first qualifying sale, and 7-day influencer activation. Track source using consent-respecting campaign tags.

## Release safeguards
1. Confirm existing live route names and signup flow.
2. Verify current Influencer Terms and all marketing claims.
3. Add page and tools without changing commission logic.
4. Mobile QA and signup/referral end-to-end testing.
5. Require explicit approval before production deployment.
